Prabhas Raju:

Prabhas is known for his charismatic screen presence and versatility as an actor. He is often referred to as the "Rebel Star" due to his daring and charismatic on-screen presence. Prabhas is also known for his private nature and dedication to his craft, which has earned him a loyal fan base.

Dr.A.P.J.Abdul Kalam:

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam (1931-2015), India's 11th President (2002-2007), renowned scientist, and "Missile Man" for his work in missile and space(ISRO/DRDO), known for inspiring youth and books like Wings of Fire; he was a pivotal figure in India's defense and space programs.

Rohit Sharma:

Rohit Sharma is an Indian cricket superstar known as the "Hitman," famous for his powerful right-handed batting, captaining India in all formats, and holding records like three ODI double centuries, most centuries in a single World Cup (5 in 2019), and the highest ODI score (264).
